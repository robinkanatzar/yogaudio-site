Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: Yogaudio does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. What you create in the app stays on your iPhone.

Yogaudio is made by Robin Kanatzar (“I” and “me” on this page). This policy covers the Yogaudio iPhone app and this website, yogaudio.app.

What stays on your iPhone

The app saves these things only on your device, so it can work the way you set it up:

  • Sessions you build, generate or save, and when you last played them.
  • Poses you mark as favorite or avoid, and any names or instructions you change.
  • Your settings, such as voice, speaking speed and how much the app says.

I cannot see any of this. It is never sent to me or to anyone else. It is removed when you delete the app. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, it is included in that backup, which Apple’s privacy policy covers.

Camera

The optional “Check my pose” feature uses the camera to compare the shape of your body with the pose. This happens entirely on your iPhone, using Apple’s built-in Vision framework. The camera picture is not recorded, saved or sent anywhere. The app asks for camera permission only when you open this feature, and you can turn it off at any time in the Settings app under Privacy & Security, Camera.

Spoken instructions

Instructions are read aloud with the voices built into iOS. Speech is produced on your iPhone and no text is sent anywhere.

No internet connection needed

Yogaudio works fully offline. The app does not connect to any server run by me or by third parties.

Purchases

Yogaudio is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment and never shares your payment details with me. Apple’s own privacy policy applies to your App Store account.

If you email me

If you write to me, for example to join the beta, report an accessibility issue or ask for help, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. The accessibility report in the app fills in your app version, iOS version, device model and whether VoiceOver is on, so I can reproduce problems. You can see and edit all of it before you send.

I use your email only to reply to you and to improve Yogaudio. I do not add you to a mailing list, sell your information or share it with anyone. If you join the beta, Apple’s TestFlight service will email you an invitation, which Apple’s privacy policy covers. Ask me at any time and I will delete our emails.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep technical logs, including your IP address, to run and secure its service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Children

Yogaudio collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date at the top. If the app ever starts handling your data differently, I will say so here before that version is released.
