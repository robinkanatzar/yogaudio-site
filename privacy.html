Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

In short: Yogaudio has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your sessions, poses and settings stay on your iPhone. Data leaves your phone only if you subscribe to Yogaudio Pro or use the premium voice, and then only what those features need, described below.

Yogaudio is made by Robin Kanatzar (“I” and “me” on this page). This policy covers the Yogaudio iPhone and iPad app and this website, yogaudio.app.

What stays on your device

The app saves these things only on your device:

  • Sessions you build, generate or save, and when you last played them.
  • Poses you mark as favorite or avoid, poses you create, and any names or instructions you change.
  • Photos you add to a pose you create. You choose each photo with the system photo picker, so the app never has access to the rest of your photo library.
  • Your settings, such as voice, speaking speed and how much the app says.
  • Premium voice audio that has already been downloaded, so it plays without internet next time.

I cannot see any of this. It is removed when you delete the app. If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, most of it is included in that backup, which Apple’s privacy policy covers.

The standard voice

By default, instructions are read aloud with the voices built into iOS. That speech is produced on your device and nothing is sent anywhere.

The premium voice (Yogaudio Pro, optional)

If you subscribe to Yogaudio Pro and turn on the premium voice, each sentence the app needs to say is sent the first time it is needed, to make the audio:

  • The request goes to Yogaudio’s own server, which runs on Cloudflare. It contains the sentence, the chosen voice and language, and your anonymous Yogaudio Pro ID (see “Purchases” below), which the server uses only to check that your subscription is active.
  • The server passes only the sentence, voice and language to ElevenLabs, which turns it into speech. ElevenLabs does not receive your Pro ID or your IP address.
  • The finished audio may be kept on the Yogaudio server, filed under a code made from the sentence, so the same sentence does not have to be made again for anyone. It is not linked to you.
  • The server keeps no logs of requests.

Most sentences are the app’s own instructions. If you create a pose or edit instructions, your own words are sent in the same way when the premium voice reads them, so please do not put personal information in them. Turn off “Use the premium voice” in Settings, Voice Management, and nothing is sent.

Purchases

Yogaudio is free to download. Yogaudio Pro is an optional subscription bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment and never shares your name, email address or payment details with me.

To know whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random ID that the app creates (not your name or Apple Account), your App Store purchase and subscription history for Yogaudio, and basic technical details such as app version, iOS version, country of your App Store account and IP address. It is used only to unlock Pro, restore purchases and show me totals such as how many people subscribe. It is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps.

You manage or cancel your subscription in the Settings app, under your name, Subscriptions.

Sharing a session

When you share a session’s audio or transcript, the app creates the file on your device and opens the system share sheet. You choose where it goes. Nothing is sent to me.

If you email me

If you write to me, for example to join the beta, report an accessibility issue or ask for help, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. The accessibility report in the app fills in your app version, system version, device model and whether VoiceOver is on, so I can reproduce problems. You can see and edit all of it before you send.

I use your email only to reply to you and to improve Yogaudio. I do not add you to a mailing list, sell your information or share it with anyone. If you join the beta, Apple’s TestFlight service emails you an invitation, which Apple’s privacy policy covers.

This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics and no tracking scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may keep technical logs, including your IP address, to run and secure its service. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Delete the app to remove everything stored on your device.
  • In Settings, Voice Management, “Delete downloaded premium audio” removes the premium voice audio stored on your device.
  • To have your subscription record deleted from RevenueCat, or our emails deleted, write to me and I will do it. Apple keeps its own record of App Store purchases.

Children

Yogaudio is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date at the top. If the app starts handling your data differently, I will say so here before that version is released.
