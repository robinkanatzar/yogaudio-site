Accessibility statement

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Yogaudio is made for people who practice yoga by ear, including people who are blind or have low vision. The website and the app should work for everyone, whatever device, assistive technology or settings you use.

Standard

This website aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA, and meets the stricter level AAA for color contrast and target size.

What this website does

  • Works with screen readers, with a clear heading structure, labelled page regions and a “Skip to main content” link.
  • Works with a keyboard alone, with a strong, visible focus outline.
  • Text and buttons have a contrast ratio of at least 7 to 1 in both light and dark mode.
  • Text grows with your browser or device text size, and the page fits a narrow screen at 400% zoom without scrolling sideways.
  • Follows your system settings for dark mode, increased contrast and high contrast (forced colors).
  • Buttons and links are large enough to tap easily.
  • No moving content, no autoplaying sound and no time limits.
  • Every button that opens your email app says so, and the email address is also written out in full.

How it was checked

The website is tested with automated checks (axe), keyboard only, narrow screens and zoom, dark mode and forced colors mode. The app is tested with VoiceOver, large text sizes and Apple’s accessibility audit.

Known limitations

The “Pre-order on the App Store” badge is an image supplied by Apple. It has a text alternative, but its colors cannot be changed.
